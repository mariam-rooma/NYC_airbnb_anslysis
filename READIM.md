🏙️ New York City Airbnb Price & Neighborhood Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project performs an in-depth analysis of Airbnb listings across New York City. The primary goal is to analyze pricing dynamics across different NYC boroughs, understand room type availability, and evaluate key metrics influencing the short-term rental market.

---

## 🎯 Key Objectives
- **Borough Pricing Analysis:** Comparing average listing prices across Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, Staten Island, and the Bronx.
- **Room Type Distribution:** Evaluating market share and price differences between Entire homes/apartments, Private rooms, and Shared rooms.
- **Listing Metrics:** Analyzing minimum night requirements, availability throughout the year, and host listing density.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Python (Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn):** Data cleaning, handling missing values, outlier treatment, and exploratory data analysis (EDA).
- **Power BI / Data Visualization:** Creating interactive charts and neighborhood price comparisons.
- **Git & GitHub:** Version control and documentation.

---

## 💡 Key Findings & Insights
- **Manhattan Dominance:** Manhattan commands the highest average listing prices and concentration of entire apartments.
- **Affordable Alternatives:** Brooklyn and Queens offer competitive pricing with a higher proportion of private rooms, appealing to budget travelers.
- **Pricing Drivers:** Room type and location (borough) are the strongest determinants of rental prices across NYC.
